About this little world

Real streets, miniature life. Built from OpenStreetMap roads around Kings Langley, with an illustrative traffic simulation and a little imagined scenery.

Drag to explore. Scroll or pinch to zoom. Select a landmark to find your way, or tap a vehicle to follow its journey.

With the map focused, use the arrow keys to move, + and − to zoom, and Space to pause. Press Escape to close the journey planner.

Bus routes and stops follow Intalink data. Services from different days run together here, with sped-up departures.

Traffic is simulated and does not represent live road conditions.
